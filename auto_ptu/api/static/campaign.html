Adventure guide

Play, don’t administer

  1. Choose what you do.Use the choice cards or describe any action in your own words.
  2. Move through the scene.Select your Trainer or Pokémon above the scene floor, then click or drag to a numbered glowing destination within its Speed. Select gold markers to inspect, talk, enter, shop, or uncover clues. Dark tiles are unknown; dim tiles are places you remember.
  3. Let the party answer.Play a party round and the local AI GM plus every Trainer will take a real turn.
  4. Roll when it matters.Checks, clocks, quests, factions, and journals update the persistent campaign.
  5. Continue the adventure.The next chapter opens when the party is ready, with consequences carried forward.
  6. Fight on the tactical board.Combat uses the full PTU engine; an Ollama player can control the active side.

Safety first: anyone can pause the table. The GM resumes after the group checks in.
